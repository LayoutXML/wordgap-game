WordGap: the daily alphabetical word puzzle

WordGap is a free word-guessing game. You see a lower and an upper word, and a hidden word sits alphabetically between them. Guess a valid word inside the bounds: a guess before the hidden word becomes the new lower bound, and a guess after it becomes the new upper bound. Find the hidden word in as few guesses as possible.

Play a new daily puzzle every day, replay past puzzles from the archive, or keep going in endless mode. WordGap works offline and can be installed as an app.
